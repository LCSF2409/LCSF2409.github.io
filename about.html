Our Story

Founded in 2005, Chandeliers Inc started as a small, family-owned business with a deep passion for creating elegant and inspiring lighting solutions. Driven by a commitment to quality and artistic craftsmanship, our founders brought their love for lighting design to life, focusing on details that transform spaces with timeless beauty. From these humble beginnings, we have expanded steadily, evolving into a trusted name in luxury lighting known for our dedication to excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Today, our extensive product range includes a variety of meticulously crafted chandeliers—from intricate, hand-cut crystal pieces to contemporary designs featuring eco-friendly LED technology. Each piece reflects our enduring values of quality and innovation, offering homeowners and designers alike access to an inspiring selection that combines aesthetic appeal with functional elegance. At Chandeliers Inc, we take pride not only in our products but also in fostering long-lasting relationships with our customers, who return time and again for our expertise, exceptional service, and unwavering commitment to their vision.
